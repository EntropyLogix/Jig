## Closing Summary

> crew architect role: works on proposals, writes the contract, architecture.md and subtasks, leaves an architect note; dev, audit and lead read the architecture documents of the issue and its ancestors; dev reports large unreviewed work and may propose subtasks

Add a role `crew architect ID` for the analysis stage, and make the
developer, auditors and lead read what the architect produced.

Architect:
- Runs only while the issue is `proposed` (analysis stage; `approve` means
  "implement"). Label is fixed: `architect`. Writes no code and never
  approves.
- With the person: analyses the issue and the code, then rewrites the
  description as the contract with acceptance criteria
  (`jig update ID --body -`), and writes the architecture in
  `.jig/docs/ID/architecture.md` (one current document, rewritten rather than
  appended; crew creates it from a template).
- Splits larger work into subtasks with their own contracts
  (`jig add TITLE --parent ID --body TEXT`; they start as proposals) and
  records blockers (`jig dep add`). A subtask's contract may refer to the
  parent's architecture document instead of repeating it.
- Finishes with `jig note ID --by architect "..."` saying the contract is
  ready, and tells the person which issues to approve.

How the others get it:
- `crew dev`, `crew audit` and `crew lead` list under `Read:` the issue's
  `architecture.md` and the `architecture.md` of every ancestor that has one.
- `crew dev` prints `Architect:` with the last architect note, or
  "not reviewed".

Developer (no blocking):
- If the work looks large and the architect has not reviewed it, the
  developer tells the person and asks whether to hand it to the architect
  (a person runs `jig revise ID --reason ...` for that); otherwise it
  continues.
- The developer may propose subtasks when the work has clearly separable
  parts (suggested, not required); they start as proposals and the person
  approves them.

Covers: role file, architecture template, crew script, SKILL usage, README, and
cases in crew_review_loop.
